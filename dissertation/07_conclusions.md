## 7.2 Future work (~450 w)

**Three doable items** — each concrete enough that someone could start on Monday:

1. **Measure the overlap coupling.** It is currently swept, not derived; every overlap result in
   this work is a lower bound because of that. Measuring it converts a bound into a number.
2. **Two dilution runs, x = 0.002 and x = 0.0002 at 100 nm, to bracket the floor below x = 0.01.**
   ≈ 20 minutes of compute. The present work refuses to extrapolate outside the sampled range;
   this is what would remove the refusal.
3. **Resolve the Bi M discrepancy between codes** (1.506 ± 0.020, three confirmations, cause
   unidentified). It is a disagreement between two established simulators and worth chasing on
   its own account.

**Two reserves** (mention, do not develop):
- Bi K error bars beyond first order.
- The detector window, if a code without a compiled-in ATW becomes available.

⚠ Also worth one sentence: **secondary fluorescence was never confirmed with the vendor** and
Walther's own simulations exclude it. It is the project's one genuinely open question.
