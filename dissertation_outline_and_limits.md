# Dissertation Outline & Length Requirements

*Source: University of Sheffield MSc Individual Project Handbook (Updated 29 January 2026), Section 6 and Appendix 6*

---

## Required Outline Sections

The handbook does not mandate one fixed chapter order — structure should be discussed with your supervisor, as it varies by project type (theoretical, experimental, design/simulation, etc.). However, the following sections are **mandatory** and must be included:

1. **Title page**

2. **Abstract**
   Concise summary of the problem, why it matters, the approach taken, and the results. Written for a technical audience interested in the design/implementation aspects of the project.

3. **Individual contribution**
   Maximum **1 page**, placed before the contents. Must clearly state your specific role and work — especially important if the project involved other students or a research group. Must reference any existing work/resources used (hardware, software, data, etc.).

4. **Introduction**
   Covers background, motivation, aims and objectives, and an overview of the report.
   - Includes a **Project management** sub-section: a summary of project planning, including a **revised Gantt chart** showing actual progress against the original plan.

5. **Literature review**
   May be a standalone chapter or embedded within other chapters, depending on relevance to the project type. Size varies by project — be guided by your supervisor.

6. **Main technical chapters**
   Describe the main work carried out. Should be logically divided to tell a coherent "story," with a balanced presentation of the work.

7. **Conclusions and future work**

8. **References**
   Consistent and correct referencing approach used throughout.

9. **Appendices**
   Additional information not essential to understanding the main report (e.g. extra results, code listings). Must **not** be used simply to keep the main report within the page limit — anything essential to the reader belongs in the main body. Appendices are **not marked** and do **not** count toward the page limit.

---

## Word / Page Limits

The handbook specifies a **page limit**, not a word-count limit.

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Main body maximum** | **70 pages** (guideline) |
| **Excluded from the page count** | Title page, abstract, individual contribution, acknowledgements, table of contents, list of references, appendices |
| **Penalty for exceeding the limit** | Overall mark multiplied by a factor = `1 − (pages over limit ÷ 100)` |
| **Example penalties** | 10 pages over → ×0.9 · 20 over → ×0.8 · 40 over → ×0.6 · 50 over → ×0.5 |
| **Zero mark threshold** | Report receives **zero** if it exceeds the limit by **100 pages or more** |

No word-count figure is specified anywhere in the handbook — length is controlled purely via the page limit above.

## Formatting & Submission Requirements

- Word-processed, clear and professional layout
- Standard, legible font: Times New Roman, Arial, or Calibri, approx. **11–12 point**
- **1.5 line spacing** preferred where possible
- Consistent headings/subheadings, adequate margins and white space
- Figures and tables clearly labelled and numbered
- Free from distracting colours or fonts; proofread carefully
- Submitted as a single **PDF**, maximum file size **20MB**
- Submission via **Turnitin**, electronically only (no hard copy)
- Deadline: **14:00** on the submission date
